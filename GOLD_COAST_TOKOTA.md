# 1. Brand Overview

## Mission Statement

To reshape manufacturing in Africa by transforming waste into premium handcrafted footwear, preserving Ghanaian craftsmanship, empowering young people through skills development and creating sustainable cultural experiences that generate lasting social, environmental and economic impact.

## Vision Statement

To become Africa's leading sustainable footwear and cultural tourism enterprise, inspiring a future where African craftsmanship, circular manufacturing and cultural heritage drive inclusive economic growth and global recognition.

## Brand Promise

Every use of the Gold Coast Tokota brand should reinforce:

- Sustainability
- Ghanaian craftsmanship
- Cultural heritage
- Innovation
- Entrepreneurship
- Circular manufacturing
- Excellence
- Authenticity

# 8. Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Shipping Locations

Gold Coast Tokota ships handcrafted sustainable footwear and accessories across Ghana and to selected international destinations.

## Domestic Shipping — Ghana

- Orders are processed within **48 hours** after payment confirmation.
- Standard delivery takes **1–2 business days**, depending on destination.
- Delivery is available nationwide through trusted courier partners.
- Customers receive a confirmation message and tracking information where available once their order has been dispatched.

## International Shipping

Gold Coast Tokota ships worldwide, subject to courier availability and customs regulations.

### Processing Time

**48 hours**

### Estimated Delivery

| Region | Estimated Delivery |
|---|---|
| West Africa | 5–10 business days |
| Europe | 7–14 business days |
| North America | 7–14 business days |
| Other destinations | 10–21 business days |

Delivery times may vary because of:

- Customs clearance
- Public holidays
- Courier delays

## Shipping Costs

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on:

- Delivery destination
- Package weight
- Courier rates

### Customs

Any customs duties, taxes or import fees imposed by the destination country are the responsibility of the customer.

## Handmade Products

Because products are handcrafted, slight variations in:

- Colour
- Texture
- Finish

may occur.

These variations are part of the uniqueness of each item and are **not considered defects**.

## Order Tracking

Customers receive shipping updates via:

- Email
- WhatsApp

once orders have been dispatched.

# 9. Returns & Exchanges Policy

Customer satisfaction is important to Gold Coast Tokota.

## Returns

Returns are accepted within **7 days of receiving the order** if:

- The product is defective.
- The incorrect item was delivered.
- The product arrived damaged during shipping.

## Returned Product Requirements

Returned products must:

- Be unused.
- Be in their original condition.
- Include original packaging.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are available for:

- Incorrect size, subject to stock availability
- Manufacturing defects
- Incorrect item received

### Size Exchange Shipping

Customers requesting a size exchange are responsible for return shipping unless the error was made by Gold Coast Tokota.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned:

- Custom-made sandals
- Personalized products
- Products damaged through misuse
- Clearance or sale items, unless defective

## Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within:

**7–14 business days**

Refunds use the original payment method.

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the error was caused by Gold Coast Tokota.

**WhatsApp:** `+233 257534297`

# 23. Brand Design Direction

The overall visual direction should communicate:

**Premium + African + Sustainable + Handcrafted + Modern**

Avoid designs that feel:

- Generic
- Overly corporate
- Cheap
- Visually cluttered
- Artificially "African" through excessive decorative elements
- Excessively green just because the brand is sustainable

The primary visual hierarchy should generally favour:

1. Black
2. White
3. Gold
4. Supporting earth/sustainability tones

The website should feel like a premium African craftsmanship brand rather than a generic ecommerce template.
